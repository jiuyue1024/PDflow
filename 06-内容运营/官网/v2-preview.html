The problem

The dangerous conversion is the one that looks successful.

Obvious failures are easy to catch. Silent structural errors are not: shifted rows, repeated headers, broken cross-page tables, wrapped text split into the wrong cell, or a value that quietly lands under the wrong SKU.

What PDflow is optimizing for

Reduce the time you spend hunting for silent errors after conversion.

What it will flag

Cross-page mismatches, suspicious values, repeated headers, missing fields, and other cases that deserve a second look.

The workflow

Conversion is only the middle step.

The goal is a reviewable result with provenance, not just an XLSX file.

01Extract

Recover structured tables.

02Flag

Surface suspicious data.

03Trace

Jump back to the source.

04Review

Edit, accept, or ignore.

05Export

Generate Excel after review.

Scope

One job, not another all-in-one PDF suite.

Built for

People who repeatedly turn supplier catalogs, SKU lists, quotations, and other difficult business PDFs into structured Excel — and still have to verify the result manually.

Not built for

If you mainly need merge, split, watermark, signing, PDF editing, or Word conversion, PDflow v2 is intentionally not trying to replace a full PDF suite.

Help shape the beta
