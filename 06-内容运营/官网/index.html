THE PROBLEM

The dangerous part isn’t when conversion fails.
It’s when it looks successful.

A shifted row, broken cross-page table, repeated header, or mismatched SKU can quietly make its way into Excel. The file opens. The table looks plausible. The mistake survives.

01

Silent structural errors

Rows can shift, headers can repeat as data, and wrapped text can split without an obvious failure message.

02

Manual checking becomes the real work

If you still have to compare every row against the PDF, “successful conversion” has not removed the expensive part.

03

Trust needs evidence

PDflow is being built to show what deserves a second look and point you back to the exact source.

WHO IT IS FOR

People who repeatedly extract structured data from difficult PDFs.

  • Supplier catalogs and SKU lists
  • Multi-page business tables
  • Wrapped-text and weak-boundary tables
  • Files where review time matters as much as extraction time
WHO IT IS NOT FOR

People looking for another all-in-one PDF toolbox.

PDflow v2 is not being optimized around merge, split, watermark, signing, PDF editing, or generic document conversion. There are already strong products for those jobs.

CURRENT STATUS

PDflow v2 is being rebuilt now.

I originally built PDflow as a broader PDF toolbox. It became more complete, but less differentiated. So I cut most of it and kept one problem: can difficult PDF → Excel become easier to trust?

Before rebuilding the full product, I’m testing against ugly real-world PDFs with known failure modes.

Current validation questionCan issue flagging meaningfully reduce the manual check?

If the answer is no, I want to learn that before adding more features.
